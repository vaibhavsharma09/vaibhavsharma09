### About

- AVP, Industrial & Automotive Cybersecurity at **TÜV SÜD**, where I built the product cybersecurity lab and an NABCB-accredited IEC 62443 / ISO/SAE 21434 certification practice
- ~19 years across Emerson, Yokogawa, EY, Deloitte, Accenture and TÜV SÜD — 300+ IACS sites assessed
- Senior Member of the **ISA/IEC 62443-3-2 Ed.2** drafting committee; ISA Qualified Instructor (IC-32/33/34/37)
- Co-chair, **CSA IoT Working Group** and CSA CAR WG3 (Continuous Monitoring & Auditing); CCZT exam writer, CCSK contributor
- Contributor to the **AIQI Consortium** AI Conformity Assessment use-case series
- Pursuing an **M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence** at Universität zu Lübeck — thesis on an agent-based AI layer for IoT platforms
